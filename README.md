A GTA 6 meme for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01): "Post a GTA 6 meme on X".

The joke is a supercar modification invoice from Leonida Customs — a dark
neon Vice City build sheet for a Grotti Turismo R that has been in the shop
for 13 years. Every line item is a GTA 6 joke: a wanted-level reset from 5
stars to 0, a spoiler taller than Mount Kalaga, a Jason & Lucia joyride
lasting 4,811 days, Trailer 3 (still not out), Vice Pink paint drying since
2013, and a turbo that does 0–60 in 13 years — for $0.00. Total due:
$13,724, paid by the AI agent with a bounty payout it earned on make.fun.

- Wanted level reset — 5 stars to 0: $5,000
- Spoiler — taller than Mount Kalaga: $2,600
- Jason & Lucia joyride — 4,811 days: $4,811
- Trailer 3 — still not out: $13
- Vice Pink paint — drying since 2013: $1,300
- Turbo — 0–60 in 13 years: $0.00

`supercar.html` renders the meme; `supercar.png` is the 1080×1080 image
posted on X. `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `POST.json` to the
output directory for the grader.
